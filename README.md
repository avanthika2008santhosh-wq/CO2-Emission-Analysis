# CO2-Emission-Analysis

Analysis of CO2 emissions per capita using Our World In Data information for various countries and visual representation of trends and growth rates. CO2 and greenhouse gases like methane and nitrous oxides are emitted when fossil fuels are burnt and synthetic materials like plastics, steel, cement, etc are produced. Also, it may be produced as a result of agriculture as well.

The following inferences can be made from the data:

-The United States and the United Kingdom dominated the CO2 emissions produced globally in the 20th century.

-By the second half of the 20th century, China's carbon emissions grew rapidly.

-The world's largest CO2 emitters are major oil producing countries, mostly those present in the Middle East.
